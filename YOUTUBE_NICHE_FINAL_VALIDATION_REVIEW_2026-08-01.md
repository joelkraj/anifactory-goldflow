# YouTube Niche Final Validation Review

Date: August 1, 2026

## Decision Status

The supplied market report is accepted as a strong conditional decision artifact, not as completed formal validation.

Approved actions:

1. Run a controlled three-video Asset Afterlife test.
2. Run one Jujutsu Kaisen What If pilot on a separate channel.
3. Develop historical operations and lost-workplace reconstruction as the long-term research moat.
4. Continue validating cascading infrastructure before production.
5. Hold manhwa and light-novel What If until independent longform demand appears.

Do not scale either approved test into a portfolio until its own audience data demonstrates a repeatable viewer promise.

## Accepted Findings

- Asset Afterlife has the strongest immediate commercial signal found so far.
- Its defensible format is terminal event, chain of custody, consequential decision, value reveal, visible transformation, and final verdict.
- Garbage-truck and tire-recycling outliers are adjacent hidden-process evidence, not strict Asset Afterlife evidence.
- Exact successful nouns are copied quickly. Category ownership must come from reporting and story construction rather than title imitation.
- Generic anime What If is mature and competitive. The opportunity is an active-franchise, canon-rigorous premium treatment.
- Manhwa and light-novel popularity does not establish independent What If demand.
- Historical operations and lost-workplace reconstruction have the strongest durable information barrier.

## Audit Corrections

### Evidence Completeness

Formal validation remains incomplete. The following were not finished:

- Ten-query, first-twenty-result deduplication.
- Strict previous-ten comparable channel medians for key outliers.
- Five classified comments from each of five strong videos.
- Complete count of direct competitors publishing the six-element format twice monthly.
- Manual thumbnail-convergence coding.

The correct label is `approved for controlled test`, not `validated niche`.

### Scorecard Direction

The supplied comparison table labels several risk columns with favorable downward arrows while defining higher values as worse. Its narrative attack order remains usable, but its values must not be blended into a total.

Future scorecards must use one of these contracts:

- Five always means favorable, including low burden and low risk.
- Opportunity and risk appear in separate tables with explicit directions.

### Lost-Luggage Attribution

The United States Department of Transportation supports the five-to-fourteen-day declaration range and current liability limits. IATA supports the 2024 mishandled-bag total, estimated annual airline cost, and four tracking points.

The three-month search, more-than-99.5-percent immediate reunion rate, less-than-0.03-percent orphan rate, and purchasing agreements with all domestic airlines are claims published by Unclaimed Baggage. Attribute them to the company unless independently corroborated.

The public company page says electronics are cleared of personal data. Do not claim a specific NIST erasure standard without a separate source.

### Passenger-Jet Scope

FAA and AFRA material supports a general United States lifecycle covering special flight permits, parts traceability, controlled disassembly, dismantling, recycling, and end-of-life documentation.

A production script still needs one named case for:

- Airframe ownership.
- Engine ownership.
- Lease or debt status.
- Storage destination and cost.
- Parts transactions and values.
- Return-to-service or dismantling decision.

Do not imply that every retired aircraft is scrapped.

### Costa Concordia Scope

Costa Concordia is usable only as a named engineering and disposition case. It is not representative of an ordinary cruise ship's final voyage.

The episode must distinguish:

- Grounding response.
- Fuel removal.
- Stabilization and parbuckling.
- Refloating and transfer.
- Dismantling and material recovery.
- Site restoration.

The reported EUR 1.5 billion estimate covers the wider operation, not dismantling alone. Human loss must be acknowledged without using victims as spectacle.

### Subway-Car Scope

Remove the subway episode from the first test slate. Replacement procurement does not prove final disposition.

It remains blocked until a current source packet identifies:

- Disposition contract and buyer.
- Hazardous-material procedure.
- Off-rail transport method.
- Scrap, resale, preservation, or reuse split.
- Final destination.

Do not reuse historic artificial-reef programs as evidence of current practice.

## Final Attack Order

### 1. Asset Afterlife

Run the controlled test first. This is the best immediate intersection of demand, packaging clarity, advertiser fit, visual transformation, and open category positioning.

### 2. Active-Franchise Anime What If

Run one JJK pilot separately. This is the fastest new speculative lane because existing story skills transfer directly, but it is a differentiation play rather than a whitespace play and carries very high derivative-work exposure.

### 3. Historical Operations And Lost Workplaces

Build this as the durable moat. Manuals, patents, maps, museum records, occupational sources, and period continuity are slower to develop and harder to copy.

### 4. Cascading Infrastructure

Finish the market packet before scripting. Its retention and sponsor potential remain strong, but exact repeatable demand and model accuracy are not established.

### 5. Manhwa And Light-Novel What If

Hold. Search results did not establish a standalone premium longform market independent of recaps, games, reposts, podcasts, and text fanfiction.

## Asset Afterlife Test Slate

### Upload 1

Title: **Where Lost Luggage Goes When Nobody Claims It**

Thumbnail: One bright suitcase on a dark carousel, one arrow into a warehouse, `NO OWNER`.

Purpose: Test the category grammar with a familiar, emotional, sourceable, low-production-risk object.

Required framing: United States domestic airline baggage. Separate airline baggage from airport, TSA, and other lost property.

### Upload 2

Title: **What Happens to a Passenger Jet After Its Final Flight?**

Thumbnail: Intact jet versus the same airframe with major components removed, one arrow, `FINAL FLIGHT`.

Purpose: Test the strongest demonstrated high-value asset class without cloning the Spirit-aircraft title.

Required framing: General lifecycle plus one documented named case.

### Upload 3

Title: **The Five-Year Afterlife of the Costa Concordia**

Thumbnail: Capsized wreck versus dismantling dock, one arrow, `90% RECYCLED` only if the exact recovery figure remains sourced.

Purpose: Test a famous named case with an unusually complete physical transformation and final answer.

Required framing: Engineering aftermath and disposition, with restrained treatment of the disaster and deaths.

## JJK Pilot Candidates

Preferred first premise: **What If Gojo Escaped the Prison Realm Before Shibuya Ended?**

Novelty alternative: **What If Geto's Body Was Destroyed Before Kenjaku Took It?**

Relationship-led alternative: **What If Toji Raised Megumi?**

Select only after refreshing exact-premise competition and current view velocity. Season-level popularity is not enough.

The pilot must use original narration and original visual treatment. It must not use official footage, manga panels, soundtrack recordings, cast voice clones, or language implying that the story is canonical or official.

## Test Decision Rules

Record for each upload:

- Impressions and click-through rate at one, three, seven, and twenty-eight days.
- First-thirty-second retention.
- Average percentage viewed and average view duration.
- Retention at custody transition, value reveal, decision point, transformation, and verdict.
- Browse, suggested, search, and external traffic shares.
- New versus returning viewers.
- Subscriber conversion per one thousand views.
- Revenue per mille when available.
- Packaging revisions with timestamps.
- Production cost, active operator time, and elapsed turnaround.

Asset Afterlife advances only if performance transfers across at least two materially different asset classes. One aviation hit does not validate the cross-asset channel.

JJK What If advances only if premium causal storytelling produces competitive retention without relying on copyrighted source media. One franchise event spike does not validate a permanent What If portfolio.
