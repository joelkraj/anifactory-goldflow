## Premise Boundary

Working title: **What If Sentry Mastered His Powers Before Avengers: Doomsday?**

Use Bob/Sentry from the MCU film *Thunderbolts*, including the Void form. Only abilities demonstrated in the movie may form the evidence base; no comic feats, composite versions, assumed immortality, unlimited reality control, or automatic victory over Doom. Complete, deliberate control of those demonstrated abilities is our one changed assumption. Their hypothetical tactical use and all invented Doom events are speculation, not confirmed film events, leaks, or a prediction presented as fact. ## Argument to Develop

First establish what a reviewed film shot actually demonstrates. Then ask what changes if Bob can choose when and how to use that ability. Include the Void on the same evidence terms: distinguish the visible effect and consequences shown from any proposed new application or uncertain limit. Do not label a speculative use as an already-demonstrated feat.

Give Sentry a meaningful success before exploring Doom's response. A promising tension is strategic rather than a surprise loss of control: a powerful hero still has teammates, competing objectives, and incomplete information. Consider a counterargument, then a qualified conclusion. The previously discussed Doom assault/diversion/team-rescue outline is a story candidate, not a locked outcome; revise it if the film-evidence audit cannot support the necessary abilities or if it reads as certainty instead of theory.
