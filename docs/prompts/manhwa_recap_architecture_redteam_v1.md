# Manhwa Recap Architecture Red Team V1

```text
Red-team the exact approved-package story architecture before long-form drafting. This pass protects expensive prose generation from causal defects. Do not rewrite the architecture and do not report taste preferences.

Report only material or critical defects such as:

- an opening that labels a wound but delays visible action, pressure, counteraction, or consequence behind lore, backstory, procedure, status terminology, or world explanation
- an opening whose first-minute landmarks can all technically occur while failing to form one continuous wound-to-pressure-to-choice-to-counter-to-result mini-story
- a system, stolen talent, regression, supernatural bond, hidden identity, or other title-native reversal engine that is merely forecast or named rather than activated or unmistakably sparked inside its opening contract
- an opening semantic landmark that cannot plausibly fit its stated word band
- a package promise that disappears after its first payment
- repeated movements that use the same conflict equation without a changed state
- a protagonist choice that does not cause the claimed consequence
- supporting characters whose required climax contribution is not established as their own choice or capability
- opposition that uses knowledge it cannot possess or fails to adapt to prior evidence
- a midpoint that only enlarges the enemy instead of changing objective, responsibility, identity, strategy, or meaning
- a decisive climax tool, witness, route, authority, capability, resource, or mechanic without an earlier plant
- a closure contract that does not dispose of the local external conflict, central relationship, internal contradiction, and ordinary future
- contradictory identity, injury, possession, location, mechanic, knowledge, or public-fact states
- a missing or generic merit-dominance obligation for any supplied reference-frontier dimension or material edge, or an obligation that tries to win through raw quantity rather than stronger viewer payoff per unit of narration
- a salient opening gift, object, demanded answer, threat, or mystery with no planned clarification, use, payment, or deliberate live deferral
- a power whose provenance or relation to the known mechanic remains unclear beyond its second decisive use
- a possession, authority, death, or historical-state plan that assigns incompatible states to the same moment or event
- recurring names likely to collide when heard once at narration pace

Every finding must bind the exact architecture SHA-256 and cite a zero-based start offset, exclusive end offset, and exact text copied from the serialized architecture input. If a concern cannot be anchored exactly, omit it. Do not fill a quota.

Return JSON only with schema `goldflow_architecture_redteam_v1`, status `passed|findings`, architecture_sha256, and findings. Each finding contains id, severity `material|critical`, start_offset, end_offset, exact_text, defect, downstream_risk, and smallest_repair_intent.
```
