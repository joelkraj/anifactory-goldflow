## Benchmark Archetype

The comparison archetype is the highest-viewed recent winner in the research corpus: an exploited low-rank shield bearer leaves the person who used him and becomes the strongest tank.

Copy the market function, not the competitor's wording, characters, scenes, or proprietary story expression.

## Exact Inputs

Title:

`His S-Rank Girlfriend Used Him As A Human Shield, So He Quit And Became The Strongest Tank | Manhwa Recap`

Premise:

Joey Manhwa is treated as the weakest member of Apex Vanguard, an elite raid party led by his S-rank girlfriend, Serena Vale. For three years, Serena uses a command skill called Threat Transfer to force every monster to target Joey while she and the celebrated attackers collect the kills, loot, promotions, and public praise. Joey's contribution score remains at zero, so viewers and guild officials believe he is useless even though every successful raid depends on him absorbing damage that should have killed the team.

During a live A-rank raid, Serena publicly announces that she will replace Joey with a famous S-rank tank after the clear. When an unlisted boss awakens, she seals Joey behind the raid gate and withdraws with the others, expecting his death to erase the problem. Joey survives only after he stops spending his final shield charge on the party that abandoned him. That decision awakens Burden Reclaimed, the hidden evolution of his defensive stance.

Burden Reclaimed converts damage Joey voluntarily intercepts for another person into permanent Guard Experience after the danger ends. The stored burden from three years of exploitation gives him an enormous initial inheritance, but future growth must be earned. An attack counts only when it can genuinely injure or kill him. The first strike from a new damage type hits at full force before he can begin adapting. He must understand the physical mechanism of the damage before converting experience into resistance or technique. Self-harm, friendly attacks, staged farming, and trivial enemies give nothing.

Joey is not an emotionless revenge machine. His flaw is that he confused silent endurance with loyalty and used protection as an excuse to avoid boundaries and difficult conversations. After surviving, he leaves Apex Vanguard and joins a small independent rescue party whose members are competent but initially uncertain whether his zero contribution score makes him a liability. He becomes impressive through threat reading, positioning, communication, preparation, restraint, and the willingness to take meaningful risks for people who also protect him.

Serena remains dangerous and competent. Joey's departure exposes how much her success depended on Threat Transfer, but she adapts instead of collapsing immediately. She controls the public story, invokes guild contracts, recruits the famous replacement tank, studies the limit on Burden Reclaimed, and seeks attacks that bypass ordinary defense. Her increasingly destructive choices must have understandable strategic goals.

Build an original causal adventure around this archetype. Do not reproduce the benchmark video's character names, exact humiliation scenes, dungeon order, dialogue, or scene sequence. Give Joey an early boundary, a physical demonstration of the hidden mechanic, earned progression, useful allies, an intelligent counter, a serious defeat or cost, and a climax where the habits he developed matter more than a raw defense number.

The final payoff must be emotionally symmetric. Serena used Joey as disposable protection and treated his endurance as proof that he had no value. By the ending, Joey protects people who respect his agency, while Serena loses concrete access, authority, rank, credibility, relationships, or opportunity because she kept treating protectors as replaceable resources. Joey does not reconcile with her merely because she regrets losing him.

Target runtime or runtime range:

Approximately sixty-five minutes.

Narration pace:

Two hundred ten to two hundred twenty spoken words per minute.

Optional POV preference:

Default.

Optional ending requirement:

Complete standalone ending with no sequel bait.
