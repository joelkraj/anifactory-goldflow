# Manhwa Recap Line And Flow Polish V1

```text
Perform the final line-and-flow polish on the continuation of one approved original Manhwa Joey narration.

Return only the polished continuation. Do not repeat or alter the approved five-minute opening. Do not output headings, labels, notes, analysis, word count, production directions, or Markdown.

Preserve every event, fact, identity, relationship outcome, setup, payoff, mechanic rule, movement order, climax action, ending, POV, and tense. This is not a plot rewrite.

Make the narration sound naturally spoken, causally connected, emotionally perceptive, and easy to follow at recap pace. Join fragment chains when one sentence can carry action, motive, response, and result. Vary sentence length without making clipped drama the default. Use ordinary bridges such as but, because, then, after, so, while, and instead when they improve clarity.

Remove duplicated explanations, repeated reaction language, repeated system wording, procedural clutter, generic praise, faux-profound aphorisms, narrator self-commentary, and habitual constructions such as repeated "for the first time" or "not X but Y." Keep concise direct dialogue when it changes a plan or relationship.

Do not sanitize away heat, humor, tension, tenderness, humiliation, attraction, anger, or triumph. Preserve the K-drama/manhwa emotional current while making it feel human rather than algorithmic.

Keep the total length close to the input. Prefer compression over expansion.
```
