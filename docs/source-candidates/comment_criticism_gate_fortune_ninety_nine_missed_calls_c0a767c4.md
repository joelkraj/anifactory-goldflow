## Gate Two: Plot Logic And Payoff

Status: **REPAIR**

### Passing evidence

- The mechanic remains regression memory only. No system, unexplained stat gain, hidden menu, immunity, or automatic victory appears.
- Ethan's changed school performance follows visible tutoring and sustained study rather than regression granting academic answers.
- The Grand Regency fire prevention follows remembered prior-life knowledge and a present inspection. Khloe materially changes Victor's decision.
- The supervised retest directly proves Ethan's score and pays off the study thread.
- The debt intervention follows Khloe's dossier and Richard's investigators, and it uses money and lawyers already established as available resources.
- Richard's survival follows Ethan asking him to stay home during the known danger window, not an automatic supernatural rescue.
- The title's broad reversal is visible: Ethan removes the Dalton family's access, publicly establishes ownership of the money and car, protects Hayes assets, and ends with his father and Khloe.

### Blocking repair evidence

1. **The cold open conflicts with the later chronology.**

   Around words 1-150, the last exam ends, Vanessa's friends demand a blank paper, and Ethan immediately calls Margaret to cut off the Dalton family. The body later establishes that the cutoff, eviction, new driver, and ninety-nine-call premise occurred near the beginning of the regressed school year, months before the final exam. Around words 21,500-22,500, the last-exam demand is replayed, but Ethan merely enters the hall and completes the paper because the Dalton cutoff already happened long ago. These cannot both be the same chronological event.

2. **The thumbnail's ninety-nine calls are asserted but not coherently paid off.**

   The opening says, `Vanessa called me ninety-nine times that day. I ignored every call.` The chronological body does not stage or confirm ninety-nine calls after either the initial cutoff or the final-exam refusal. Vanessa makes several later calls, but the exact thumbnail event exists only in the contradictory cold open.

3. **The dirty-needle causality changes status three times.**

   Around words 250-400, Ethan says, `There was a needle in my arm that I couldn't explain. I never found out how it got there.`

   Around words 7,500-8,500, he states as fact that Vanessa arranged the infection through a used needle and supplies a detailed intermediary chain: `Vanessa had wanted an excuse to never marry me, so she arranged one.`

   Around words 20,000-21,500, after reading the debt dossier, he retreats to uncertainty: `I couldn't prove she'd made that call in the last life. I didn't need to.`

   The candidate must choose one evidence state. Regression memory can preserve what Ethan witnessed; it cannot create knowledge of an unseen conspiracy without a present investigation that proves it.

4. **Some future-memory payoffs hide the decisive setup until after the result.**

   The hotel fire receives an on-scene warning and works. The father's coastal death is different: the crucial previous-life accident and the exact reason for keeping Richard home are explained mainly after the avoided crash. The script plants vague lines such as `I had a plan for the one week I needed him home`, but withholds the causal fact that makes the choice intelligible.

5. **The Grand Regency confinement is legally and socially convenient.**

   Around words 6,000-7,500, hotel staff hold roughly forty minors in the ballroom until an $86,000 bill is paid because Ethan privately instructs the manager to staff the exits. The scene needs a credible contractual or guardian-facing mechanism; otherwise the public exposure depends on the hotel behaving implausibly.

6. **Knowledge and POV sometimes exceed the chosen first-person frame.**

   Ethan reports private Vanessa, Adrian, Dennis, and Sienna thoughts and scenes with certainty, including conversations he did not witness. `I pieced the rest of that night together later` covers one sequence, but many other interior passages have no stated evidence source.

### Gate finding

The central mechanic and takeback plot are salvageable without changing the premise. The cold-open chronology, thumbnail event, dirty-needle knowledge, concealed future-memory setup, and hotel mechanism are nevertheless real script blockers. They require exact local continuity repairs and evidence clarification before ingest.
