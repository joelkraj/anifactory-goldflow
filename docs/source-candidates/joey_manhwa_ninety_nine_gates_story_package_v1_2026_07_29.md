## World and Cast

The country depends on ninety-nine S-rank portal gates for travel, emergency evacuation, energy routing, and defense. Every gate has a spectacular public ring and an ugly manual safety layer that most engineers ignore.

- Joey Manhwa: F-rank Maintenance engineer. Quiet, observant, physically capable, and under-credited. He designed and installed the local breaker inside every S-rank gate.
- Emily Hart: Joey's fiancée at the opening and director of Crown Transit. Brilliant at politics, capital, and public messaging. She values systems she can own.
- Jace Carter: S-rank Vector Engineer and Emily's new partner. Genuinely talented, proud, fast, and dangerously convinced that central control can replace local judgment.
- Nora Vale: B-rank resonance engineer. She understands gate materials, challenges Joey, and becomes the technical lead.
- Marcus Reed: A-rank rescue captain. He owns field command whenever lives are at risk.
- Lena Ortiz: B-rank medic and signal officer. She owns injury limits, pulse timing, and civilian status.
- Theo Park: C-rank route runner. He maps physical access, moves parts, and coordinates local crews faster than official channels.

Joey and Nora may develop warmth and trust, but the story does not require a replacement romance.

## Canonical Mechanics

### Gate Architecture

Each S-rank gate contains:

1. A public portal ring that carries the main load.
2. A local anchor that matches the site.
3. A manual breaker that isolates one gate from the national network.
4. A route key held by the local crew.

Joey designed the breaker layer. Crown Transit kept his class and title low because Maintenance work did not generate glamorous clear records.

Jace's `Crown Sync` patch removes local delay by coupling all ninety-nine anchors to one central control core. Joey refuses to certify it because one bad phase command can propagate across the entire network. Jace installs it under Emily's director authority after she fires Joey.

### Joey's Evolution

Joey's F-rank Maintenance class evolves into `World Architect` only after he refuses the false certification and physically saves people during the first rupture.

The evolution provides tools, not decisions:

- `Fault Sight` shows current stress only in a structure Joey physically inspects and substantially understands.
- `Foundation Marks` form only after a repair survives its real load and the protected people are safe.
- A mark can reinforce only a structure Joey or a consenting teammate physically built, repaired, and tested.
- `Route Share` unlocks only after multiple local key holders voluntarily accept independent responsibility. Any holder can sever their own link.

Hard limits:

- Joey has no remote omniscience.
- He receives no blueprint for unknown material or gate behavior.
- He cannot create gate energy.
- He cannot override ownership, steal a route key, or force consent.
- He cannot disable, reclaim, or sabotage work built by someone else.
- A current crisis cannot reward the repair before the danger ends.
- One central Foundation Mark cannot stabilize the whole country.

### Jace's Strength

Jace's S-rank Vector Engineer class can calculate and redirect enormous portal loads in real time. He can repair visible phase errors much faster than Joey. His weakness is not incompetence. He models the network as one controllable machine and discounts local differences he did not personally inspect.
