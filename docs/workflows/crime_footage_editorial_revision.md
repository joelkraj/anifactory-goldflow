# CrimeDungeon editorial revision

An authorized editorial rebuild uses a new immutable identity and the separate `crime_footage_editorial_proof_v1` controller. Its private 1080p30 proof is explicitly 60–90 seconds; it does not pad the earlier 90–150 second format or modify that frozen workflow. Lock the new title, case argument, exact narrator bridges, source selection and execution instruction before work. Execution authorization remains separate from script, listening and program approval.

The source-only producer `produceCrimeFootageEditorialSources({proofDir,attemptToken,recipePath})` in [crime-footage-editorial-sources.mjs](../../scripts/lib/crime-footage-editorial-sources.mjs) runs inside a fresh guarded `source_assets` attempt. Bind its code, recipe, prior identity and completed prior source-stage receipt before dispatch. Its recipe schema is `crime_footage_editorial_source_reuse_v1`, scope `same_case_verified_source_windows_new_editorial_proof`, with `from_proof_dir`, `to_proof_dir`, exact `from_identity` and `from_source_stage` references, `selected_windows:[{source_id,window_id}]`, the exact new identity's `authorization_instruction`, and a specific `review_note`.

The new selection may be a subset of the earlier proof, but each copied source ID, window ID, public URL and start/end bound must remain identical. Every media window selected by the new identity must be covered; this producer cannot silently leave a new acquisition outstanding. It verifies the completed source receipt, accepted file bytes and retained correction evidence, then exclusively copies those bytes below the new attempt. The Clancy V03 original-English correction and its lineage remain intact. Historical source costs remain historical; local copying makes zero provider calls and records zero new spend.

No old narration is carried into the editorial revision. The separate guarded narrator stage synthesizes the newly locked bridges through the existing pinned owned Qwen voice. No previous approval is inherited. Partial copy failures require triage; no overwrite, retry, network operation, new footage window, full production or publishing is enabled here. The existing proof-v2 technical repair remains a different, frozen workflow.

Provider-free verification: `node scripts/tests/crime-footage-editorial-sources-tests.mjs`. Its synthetic media verify byte copies, exact subset selection, identity/window refusals and immutable attempts; they do not establish real-case factual verification or listening approval.
