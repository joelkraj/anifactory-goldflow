## Sound and effects: findings versus leads

The automated analyses consistently describe close narration, rougher recorded room/location sound, a low atmospheric score, mostly direct cuts and occasional clicks, static, risers or bass accents. These are useful **listening leads**, not independently verified inventories. Review Attic 00:16–00:33 and 01:04–02:15, College 01:04–01:18 and 18:31–19:41, then a sustained source encounter and final outcome to log the actual entrances, exits and function of each layer.

Reject precise-but-unverified prescriptions: a fixed 4–7-second recut rule, a global footage percentage, a universal cyan/red factual-status code, a click on every evidence object, or the provider's proposed zoom and ducking numbers. The current evidence also does not establish VHS as a common channel treatment. A subtle archival texture for our audio card can be an original proposed design choice; it must not age a 2023 call deceptively or obscure words/disclosure.

## Five specific omissions in our latest proof

These concern the retained **editorial-v3 64.4-second** candidate. It already improved document highlighting, cue timing, independent cutout movement and an underscore with one accent. Earlier complaints about having no music at all describe pacing-v2, not the current candidate.

1. **No recorded cold-open scene.** The proof still starts with narration over a portrait/document treatment. Sinister End's night bodycam and Dr Insanity's roadside/interview openings make the scene itself the first event. A new source-led proof needs a real selected action or exchange before the first explanatory board.
2. **No audio/transcript scene of its own.** All 63.38 seconds of retained speech use the narrator, including the document reading. We have not demonstrated caller/dispatcher identification, line-timed transcript changes, active-speaker emphasis, waveform, persistent AI-recreation labeling or a handoff back to footage. This needs its own designed scene and checked text/voice inputs, rather than adding a waveform to narration.
3. **No functional footage inset returning to full frame.** Our two-person cutout and paired-card layouts cannot demonstrate what Sinister End does at 19:58 → 20:03: relate a moving recording to geography, then restore visual immersion. A representative proof should contain that exact type of compositional change with supported geography.
4. **No alternation between source sound and authored sound.** The new bed and accent sit under the same narrator; there is no demonstrated field/interview sound perspective, narrator entrance after an answer, or score reduction that hands a consequential source moment to its original audio. The next mix needs a logged source-speech/ambience/music handoff, not simply additional effects.
5. **No representative transition into a completed segment payoff.** The minute ends on a narrated next-search bridge and cannot demonstrate a real encounter resolving, a date/location reset into another source scene, or the eventual switch to verified outcome treatment. A future proof should complete one local question with a source-backed payoff, then show one motivated transition. Full-case conclusion remains a separate edit requirement.

## Minimum reference log for subsequent direct checks

For each representative passage, record: exact upload/time; what is on screen; source/caption label; observed movement and transition; speech speaker and entry/exit; ambience, music and effect entrance/exit; what each treatment helps the viewer understand; and whether it was directly viewed/listened to or remains provisional. Record sound and picture separately. A screenshot supports composition; continuous viewing/listening supports choreography and sound.
