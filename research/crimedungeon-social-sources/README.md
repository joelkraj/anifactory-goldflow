**Status: source lead; case details, outcome and reuse basis unresolved.** Submitted by the user, inspected September 8, 2026. No media acquired or approved for production.

- Discovery: [X post by Body Cam Cops, @VnMn22019093](https://x.com/VnMn22019093/status/2095542604322742400). Caption: “Cop Catches Student Stealing His OWN Booted Tire!” The page displayed September 3, 2026 as its posting date. This caption is a publisher's framing, not our finding.
- The visible Community Note linked to [California Bodycam's YouTube presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8OHRPzJpAs), titled **20-Year-Old Student Charged with Theft After Caught Removing Tire with Parking Boot**. The YouTube page displayed a duration of **26:39** and premiere date **September 30, 2024**.
- That upload's description attributes the incident to **June 10, 2024**, University of Cincinnati Police Department. This attribution has not been independently checked. The Community Note's assertion about dismissed charges also remains unverified.
- The post's advertised “full parts” destination used `foxnews.briefix.net`, not `foxnews.com`; the Community Note warned of deceptive Fox News branding. That destination was not visited or audited. The note's separate YouTube lead was followed instead.
- The longer upload remains a third-party presentation, not an established agency master or unedited recording. YouTube also lists a music track; source audio needs a separate review before excerpt selection.

**Review performed:** public X post text, visible Community Note, and expanded YouTube metadata/description. X video playback required login and was not inspected. Neither end-to-end video review nor frame matching between the X clip and YouTube upload was completed. The YouTube player was paused after inspection. No original-recording ownership or primary court outcome was established.

**Editorial potential:** an immediately understandable disagreement about removing a booted wheel could support an encounter-focused story if the recording, charge history and outcome check out. No title or allegation is adopted from the repost. Next research would establish the incident identifier and primary outcome, trace the underlying recording, then log useful time windows and the applicable use basis. A recent repost of older material can reveal an available story; it does not establish a new incident.
