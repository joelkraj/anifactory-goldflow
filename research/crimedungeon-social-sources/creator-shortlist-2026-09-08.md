# CrimeDungeon: social creators that can lead to longer source material

Research date: 2026-09-08. Bounded public-web review for the archive-led hybrid format. This is a discovery shortlist, not a footage clearance or production manifest. No media was downloaded, accounts followed, subscriptions purchased, requests sent, or automations created.

**Start with Code Blue Cam and Midwest Safety for bodycam case discovery, and Court TV for clips that have a clear route into longer proceedings.** Keep Law&Crime, EWU and Real World Police as supporting libraries. The useful unit is a case with an identifiable longer source, rather than an isolated viral clip. The ranking below reflects the source paths found in this sample; it does not establish an ongoing success rate.

## How to turn this into a useful intake list

1. **Capture the discovery item and the case separately.** Save the exact social permalink, account, observed date, post date, any source credit and the hook. Create or attach to one case record using incident date, jurisdiction and a stable public case identifier where available. Upload date is not incident date.
2. **Resolve the longer source before ranking the package.** Follow the creator's own links; search exact phrases plus named location/agency; then locate any existing official release or full proceeding. A good short with no identifiable case remains a discovery lead, not an asset ready to edit.
3. **Score the route, then the story.** Proposed 10-point triage: origin trace 0–3, longer usable coverage 0–3, document/outcome support 0–2, specific narrative question and reversal 0–2. This is an editorial rubric, not a measured creator rating. Preserve “unknown” when an item has not been checked. Prefer cases with several meaningful scenes already available.
4. **Keep audio and picture provenance separate.** Record whether the clip carries original incident audio, publisher narration, music, reaction audio or a synthetic recreation. Keep the source's captions and overlays marked as publisher additions. A clean-looking picture does not establish clean source audio.
5. **Collapse reposts into the same recording family.** Link derivative clips to their earliest verified source; compare spoken lines, source timestamps, camera angle and watermarks. Ten reposts of one camera recording are one source family. Court TV/Law&Crime and the EWU channels also need publisher-family links.
6. **Use the short to choose the scene, the longer recording to understand it.** For a vertical-only source, use a readable portrait panel within the 16:9 CrimeDungeon layout alongside documents, dates or location context. Avoid cropping away people, source timestamps or context to fill the frame. Choose cutout recreations only where the hybrid format calls for an explicitly identified reconstruction.
7. **Measure the first small batch instead of assuming reliability.** Review five recent discovery items each from the first three publishers. Record how many resolve to a named case, longer source, reviewable audio and sufficient supporting records, plus research minutes per success. Promote creators by that observed yield. This is a proposed manual evaluation, not a scheduled monitor.

## Verification limits and exclusions

- Public-web metadata and primary publisher pages were used. Some YouTube and X pages returned thin text or access errors; no claim is made that the videos were watched end-to-end. Search snippets and cached indexes can lag the research date.
- TikTok **@courttvlive** was verified through a publisher social directory. Other proposed TikTok handles were omitted where first-party affiliation was not established. No individual TikTok post was inspected here.
- The X handle **@Police_Activity** was not established as the official PoliceActivity YouTube account. Do not add it under that identity based on its name alone. PoliceActivity remains a possible YouTube catalogue to review separately; it was not promoted to the six-account shortlist without a stronger verified social route.
- Creator footage is a discovery asset until the exact proposed use and provenance have been reviewed under the hybrid format. Existing public access does not by itself clear the creator's narration, music, graphics or compilation. Where only the creator's edited version exists, record that limitation rather than calling it an agency master.
- This research does not change the true-crime workflow's design-only status, approve an episode, authorize voice cloning, or authorize uploads. It supplies a bounded shortlist for building an archive-first case queue.
