# Dr Insanity reference evidence audit

September 9, 2026. Research-only review of retained records, not a new viewing/listening session. No external credits, downloads, episode commands or modifications to earlier artifacts. Read the current AGENTS router and CrimeDungeon hybrid/private-proof guidance.

## Finding

The retained research supports a repeatable editorial construction and a concrete visual vocabulary. It does **not** yet establish a complete measured sound/motion specification or prove what causes the channel's retention. The central construction is **consequential recorded encounter → brief orientation → developed exchange → evidence that changes its meaning → next encounter → source payoff and later outcome**. Treat that as a decision system, not a fixed sequence of decorative templates.

Evidence classes below: **F** = earlier researcher recorded an actual loaded player-frame check; **C** = earlier researcher read native timestamped captions; **P** = automated audiovisual analysis only. This audit inspected those written records, not the referenced frames anew. Caption boundaries are approximate, and a sustained source-audio passage is not necessarily one uncut shot.

## Package evidence

The retained September 8 live-channel study inspected 12 Latest thumbnail/title pairs individually: **11/12 had no added headline, 0/12 evidence insets or obvious panel collages, 12/12 people within a scene, 12/12 recorder-style decoration.** Titles were 7–10 space-separated words, used roles/scenarios instead of full case names, and none was phrased as a question. See [packaging study](../dr-insanity-format-2026-09-08/packaging-study.md).

This supports a source-scene-first thumbnail default. The previously proposed Patrick + pharmacy inset and named TikTok-question title are original package choices, **not** a direct match to that observed channel pattern. They may fit this premise, but should earn selection over a simpler authentic single scene. Recorder symbols in competitor thumbnails do not establish source authenticity or justify fabricated bodycam metadata. Public views do not establish thumbnail CTR, retention or why a package succeeded.

## Corrections and unsupported precision

- **College 80.6% source audio:** contradicted by captions in the specified 15:20–18:20 window; rejected. The retained approximate partition is 15s narrator + 104s source + 55s narrator + 6s non-speech = 180s. This is a local caption-based estimate, not a whole-film quota.
- **Attic 75% source audio:** approximately matches one caption-derived three-minute sample (136s source/44s narration), but supplies no channel-wide percentage.
- **All narration bridges are 5–10 seconds:** contradicted by Cold case's roughly 30-second 42:14–42:44 bridge and longer setup passages elsewhere.
- **Recut every 4–7 seconds:** provider suggestion, not measured behavior. Long dialogue blocks alone do not disprove rapid picture cutting; source-audio duration and shot duration must be measured separately.
- **Attic call is raw 911:** contradicted by the directly checked recreation label. Later provider claims also misplaced that label and the call sequence: actual sampled 01:47 contains a tilted inset and 01:57 has returned to full source footage.
- **Exact bass hits, risers, clicks, whooshes, score ducking, filtered speech, zoom speed and cut types:** predominantly P, without independent listening or continuous-motion confirmation. Agreement between automated reports does not promote them to direct observation. Their 2–5% zoom, 3–5dB ducking and 0.5–1s transition suggestions are proposed settings, not measured reference parameters.
- **VHS/scanlines, universal factual-status colors, precise [LIE] badges, synchronised multiple insets:** not established by retained direct checks. Do not put them into a reference-derived rule.
- **Objective narrator:** inaccurate characterization; caption reviews recorded mind-reading, demeanor interpretations and moral judgments. We can adopt evidentiary progression while writing more precisely.

## Highest-value missing evidence

1. **Direct continuous picture-and-sound review** of a compact comparison set: each opening, one developed exchange, an evidence transition and the conclusion. Prior logs are snapshots/captions plus automated whole-video reports. Log actual entrances/exits, cuts, score, ambience and effects separately; do not claim listening from captions.
2. **A complete question/payoff map** for at least one film, then cross-check a second. Existing windows identify the mechanism but cannot give full-film segmentation, dropped questions, repeated teases or payoff latency.
3. **A real package-to-opening comparison:** exactly what the title/thumbnail promises, where the opening establishes it, where the film delivers it. No sample presently binds these into an assessed contract.
4. **Actual artifact comparison at normal playback and phone size.** Static same-camera coverage is not automatically poor; it fails when the exchange stops developing or overlays obscure it. Check understanding, source/narrator handoff and payoff in our cut rather than rewarding number of effects.
5. **Performance measurement after release.** Define our editorial pass criteria now, but reserve claims about beating retention/CTR for observed results. Neither popularity nor style resemblance proves that outcome.

Recommended durable rule: require every episode's paper edit to identify its viewer promise, selected opening source window, question/evidence/payoff for each segment, presentation and sound functions, source limitations, and conclusion. Require an independent review of the actual candidate against those fields before showing a purported improvement. This prevents another polished explanatory insert from being mistaken for proof of the entire format.
