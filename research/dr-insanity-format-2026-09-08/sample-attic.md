## Method

A full-video vidIQ analysis was completed. Independently read the native timestamped captions across the opening, roadside encounter, tactical sequence and ending, and inspected actual loaded player frames at **00:35, 01:55, 15:20, 19:32, 19:46 and 33:49**. The last frame was partly obscured by page scroll and end-screen recommendations; it supports only a limited observation of a portrait/outcome layout.

This was automated full-video analysis plus independent caption/frame checks, not end-to-end listening or a shot-by-shot motion review. Approximate speech boundaries come from captions and context. Sound descriptions remain provider impressions. Full captions, reference-video media and third-party frames are not copied into this packet.

## Picture and sound

At **00:35**, an actual roadside frame has white, two-line dialogue text on dark rectangular backing, with some sensitive wording masked. At **15:20**, dialogue text is cyan. These demonstrate large authored captions and color variation, not a verified universal speaker-color mapping. Source environment remains legible; dramatic usefulness does not require a perfect portrait crop.

The provider describes a close, low male narrator, restrained dark musical texture and preserved location sound. That is useful creative direction for later listening, but no narrator speed, audio level, EQ curve, score timing or cut frequency was independently measured. Our eventual mix should be reviewed on its own audio, with source-speech intelligibility as the objective.

## Corrections to automated observations

- **The early call is labeled an audio recreation.** The provider described it as raw 911 audio. The visible label overrides that classification.
- The provider's roughly 75% source-audio estimate happens to align with one caption-derived **15:00–18:00** sample: about 136 seconds source exchange and 44 seconds narration. Caption boundaries are approximate and do not measure all nonverbal gaps. This is a local scene, not a whole-video/channel ratio or production quota.
- A reported aerial-map moment around 25:54 was not independently confirmed as such; the caption context is a tactical drone search. Do not use that provider timestamp as verified map evidence.
- The sampled 19:32 graphic verifies the desktop-window motif. It does not authenticate the depicted database or fields as an agency screenshot.

The reusable construction is **recorded encounter → orientation → developed exchange → evidence explanation → next encounter**, with a later-moment cold open and a clearly dated reset. Suspense comes from the unfolding search and account testing. Claims about a person's private thoughts are not established by this editing pattern.
