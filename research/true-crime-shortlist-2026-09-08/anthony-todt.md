## Case and outcome

Anthony John Todt was convicted of four counts of first-degree murder and animal cruelty in the deaths of Megan, Alek, Tyler, Zoe, and the family dog. On April 14, 2022, Judge Keith Carsten imposed four consecutive life sentences without parole plus one year for animal cruelty. This is confirmed by the prosecutor's official release. [Ninth Circuit State Attorney](https://sao9.net/state-attorney-worrell-announces-conviction-of-anthony-todt-for-first-degree-murder-and-cruelty-to-animals/)

Primary judicial text located: Sixth DCA **6D23-1278**, lower tribunal **2020-CF-149-A-O-S**, affirmed April 18, 2023. The actual court opinion is reproduced by Justia; it includes a rehearing-finality notice. This pass did not exhaust later postconviction filings or establish the latest mandate status. [Court opinion PDF](https://cases.justia.com/florida/sixth-district-court-of-appeal/2023-6d23-1278.pdf?ts=1713369942) The predecessor appeal and transfer are independently traceable in the [official Fifth DCA docket](https://onlinedocketsdca.flcourts.org/DCAResults/CaseByYear?CaseNumber=994&CaseYear=2022&Court=5).

## Proposed package and progression

- Title A: **His Confession Reached the Jury. Then He Blamed His Wife.**
- Title B: **Anthony Todt: The Recording and the Story He Told in Court**
- Thumbnail: a licensed/original interview still paired with the actual courtroom close-up, caption **TWO ACCOUNTS**. No fabricated expression, deceased-person dialogue, or graphic victim image.
- Structure: short courtroom contradiction → introduce the family and discovery using sourced records → establish which police recording is being discussed → compare the accounts claim by claim → assess corroborating and conflicting evidence → verdict, sentence, and dated appeal outcome.

The two principal uploads independently establish that his recorded account and courtroom position differ. A production script must also examine why the defense challenged his earlier statements, and the rulings governing them. Do not summarize this as “confessed, therefore every detail of the confession is true.” [Interrogation upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8703tQkTQDc), [testimony upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fA9bPj-A3k)
