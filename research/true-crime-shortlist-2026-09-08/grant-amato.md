## Factual spine and status

The [Seminole County Sheriff’s original release and updates](https://www.seminolesheriff.org/output/mediareports/11839/Sultan%20Circle%20triple%20homicide.pdf) identify Chad, Margaret Ann, and Cody Winston Amato as the victims, describe the January 25, 2019 welfare check, distinguish January 26 investigative detention from the January 28 arrest, and identify the investigating agency. Use the dated update paragraphs; the PDF header appears to contain a year typo.

The judge’s recorded sentencing at 11:24–12:16 confirms three murder findings and a life-without-parole sentence on each count in Seminole case **2019-CF-337-CFA**. [Court TV’s case page](https://www.courttv.com/news/florida-man-obsessed-with-webcam-girl-on-trial-for-murder-of-family/) separately reports conviction on July 31, 2019 and sentencing on August 12, 2019. These are distinct stages; several search summaries incorrectly combine their dates.

The Fifth District’s [May 4, 2021 opinion, case 5D19-2403](https://cases.justia.com/florida/fifth-district-court-of-appeal/2021-5d19-2403.pdf?ts=1620152495) affirms the appeal. This is the court-authored primary opinion hosted by Justia, not a freshly retrieved official-host docket. A search in the [official Florida ACIS portal](https://acis.flcourts.gov/portal/search/case) for Fifth District / 2019-2403 remained at “Loading” during this pass. **No claim is made that all later collateral litigation or current custody details have been checked.** Obtain the current clerk docket and judgments before final script approval.

1. Brief opening from the changed-departure-time discussion, with dates and context.
2. Welfare check, victims, location, and the agency’s search/detention/arrest chronology.
3. His explanation for the money and household conflict; attribute his assertions.
4. Build a source-linked timeline; compare the interview account with the digital evidence presented at trial, after reviewing the actual testimony and exhibits.
5. Explain the prosecution and defense positions, the jury verdict, sentencing, and verified appeal disposition. Include the victims’ lives and the surviving family without using grief as a spectacle.
